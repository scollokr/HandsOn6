About Our Shop

First opened our doors in 1993, Bagel World has been a staple of North Shore homes, schools and offices. We take great pride in bringing our loyal customers a truly authentic bagel made with the same Polish-inspired recipe our father first created.

We believe in high quality, speed, and warmth. From early morning breakfast rushes to lazy afternoon coffee breaks, our shop is built around community and comfort.

What Makes Us Special

  • Authentic Recipe: Polish-inspired kettle-boiled dough baked fresh every morning.
  • Free High-Speed Wi-Fi: Stay connected while enjoying your morning brew.
  • Cozy Seating Area: Plentiful tables to meet up with friends or work comfortably.
  • Fast Service: Optimized counter line so you get your breakfast without the long wait.
